# Product — Pangkalan

Pangkalan is a digital ojek (motorcycle-taxi) base station for the Kiroween 2026 hackathon,
category Resurrection. It revives the traditional Indonesian pangkalan queue system:
drivers wait in fair rotation at a physical base and take turns serving passengers.

Audience: ojek drivers in Indonesia (e.g. Berau, Kalimantan Timur) using low-end phones.
UI language: Bahasa Indonesia. App language for docs/README: English.

Core value: fair rotation (no driver skipped), official fare (Kepmenhub KP 348/2019 Zona III,
ceiling Rp 2.600/km — Sambaliung–Bandara 12,5 km = Rp 32.500), and an offline-first trip log
so the pangkalan keeps its daily record without internet.
